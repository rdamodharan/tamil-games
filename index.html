📖 Self-Learning Guide: The Tamil Alphabet

Uyir Ezhuthukkal (உயிர் எழுத்துக்கள்): These are the 12 Vowels (+1 special letter ஃ). Just like A, E, I, O, U in English, these are the core sounds of the language.

Mei Ezhuthukkal (மெய் எழுத்துக்கள்): These are the 18 Consonants. You can spot them easily because they have a dot on top (e.g., க்). They act like English consonants (B, C, D) before adding a vowel.

Uyirmei Ezhuthukkal (உயிர்மெய் எழுத்துக்கள்): These 216 Combined Letters are made by blending a consonant and a vowel together (e.g., க் + அ = க). This is similar to reading syllables in English like "ba", "ki", or "mo".
